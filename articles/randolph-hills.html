Inside Randolph Hills: Jewish Business Owners on Community, Kosher Commerce, and Legacy

A shopping center in Rockville, MD where entrepreneurship and community service are one and the same.

The Advantage · January 25, 2026 · Business

Randolph Hills shopping center in Rockville, MD, has long been a hub for Jewish-owned businesses, from kosher restaurants to Judaica shops. Owners like Ramesh Zahed of Sienna's and Tracy Yitzachy of Motis Market blend entrepreneurship with community service, providing trusted kosher food, unique products, and a welcoming space for the broader Jewish community across the DMV.

These businesses thrive not just on sales, but on relationships, values, and cultural connection. For many of these owners, the business didn't start with a business plan—it started with a need they saw in their community and a willingness to fill it.

The result is a shopping center that functions as more than a commercial strip. It's a gathering place, a weekly ritual for many families, and a point of pride for a community that values both commerce and culture.

In an era when many brick-and-mortar retailers are struggling, Randolph Hills continues to draw foot traffic because it offers something online shopping cannot replicate: a sense of belonging.

The lesson for young entrepreneurs is clear. When you build for your community—when you understand your customers not just as buyers but as neighbors—you create something with a durability that market forces alone can't provide.
